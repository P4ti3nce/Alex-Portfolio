Personal project 2026

Historical Market Data Analysis Tool

A tool that tests whether simple trading rules hold up on real data. It processes 44 million one-minute price bars with its own LZMA decoder, runs each idea through out-of-sample testing and 20,000-path simulations, and checks itself against synthetic data. The best rule matched its backtest to within one trade over 16 months live on cTrader.

  • C#
  • .NET 8
  • cTrader

A seeded procedural generator that builds an endless 3D world in chunks, with five level-of-detail settings and desert, grassy and island biomes. I profiled each biome and found one variant raised CPU spikes from 0.25 ms to 4 ms and memory from 452 MB to 1.32 GB.
